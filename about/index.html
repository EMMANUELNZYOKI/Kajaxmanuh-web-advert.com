Our story

A technology partner with business in mind.

KajaxCodeLab was founded in 2024 with a simple aim: help businesses use technology to move forward. We bring together web development, software thinking and digital strategy to create useful experiences—not technology for its own sake.

We take time to understand the people, processes and goals behind each project, then recommend a clear way to make it work online.
